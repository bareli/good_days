# Good Days (ימים טובים)

Upcoming Shabbat, Jewish holidays and fasts, merged with your family calendars, with live countdowns. Hebrew and English, RTL.

- `calendar.good_days_holidays`: Shabbat / Yom Tov from candle lighting to havdalah, holidays and fasts as all-day events.
- Sensors: next Shabbat (candle lighting, havdalah, parasha), next holiday, holiday today.
- `custom:good-days-card`: merged agenda with countdowns, Hebrew dates, candle lighting, "falls on Shabbat" warnings, visual editor.
- Offline: computed locally with hdate. Israel or diaspora.

Setup: Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Good Days.
