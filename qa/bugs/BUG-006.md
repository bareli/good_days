## Summary

The options flow (Settings > Devices & services > Good Days > הגדר) describes the Shabbat hold wrongly, in
both languages. A user deciding whether to keep it on is told that a reminder due on Shabbat waits until
after havdalah. The code sends it an hour **before** candle lighting. The label says "delay"
("לעכב"), but the reminder actually goes out earlier. For a yahrzeit or birthday on Shabbat those are
opposite outcomes: one comes before Shabbat and the other after it.

## Steps to reproduce

1. As qa_admin, open Settings > Devices & services > Good Days > **הגדר**.
2. Scroll to the last toggle.

## Expected

The wording matches the behaviour recorded in CHANGELOG v0.4 ("Reminders due on Shabbat / Yom Tov are sent
an hour before candle lighting (before: held until havdalah...)"), README *Reminders*, `qa/PROJECT.md`
*Deliberate behaviour*, and `reminders.py` line 128 ("Due on Shabbat / Yom Tov: send it before candle
lighting instead").

## Actual

- he label: "לעכב תזכורות בשבת ובחג"; description: "תזכורות שחלות בשבת או בחג יישלחו אחרי ההבדלה."
- en label: "Hold reminders during Shabbat and Yom Tov"; description: "Reminders due on Shabbat or Yom Tov are sent after havdalah."

(`translations/he.json` lines 44 / 50, `translations/en.json` lines 44 / 50; generated from `scripts/gen_strings.py`.)

## Likely root cause

The v0.4 behaviour change did not update the strings in `scripts/gen_strings.py`.

## Relevant files

- `scripts/gen_strings.py` (source of `quiet_on_shabbat` label and description)
- generated `strings.json`, `translations/he.json`, `translations/en.json`

## Recommended remediation

Proposal for the owner (copy, needs a ruling):

- he label: "לא לשלוח תזכורות בשבת ובחג"
- he description: "תזכורת שחלה בשבת או בחג תישלח שעה לפני הדלקת הנרות."
- en label: "No reminders during Shabbat and Yom Tov"
- en description: "A reminder due on Shabbat or Yom Tov is sent an hour before candle lighting."

Edit `scripts/gen_strings.py` and regenerate; never hand-edit the translations.
