## Summary

WCAG 2.1 AA 1.4.4 Resize Text and 1.4.10 Reflow (no loss of content at 320 CSS px / 400% zoom). The card's `.name` is
`white-space: nowrap; text-overflow: ellipsis` and has no `title` and no other place where the full text is repeated. At 320 px the
holiday "שמיני עצרת · שמחת תורה · שבת" reads "שמיני · שמחת תור..."; the same happens at 640 px (1280 px at 200% zoom) in a
standard dashboard column. Expanding the item does not help: the details show the Hebrew date, candle lighting, havdalah and the calendar
name, not the title. In the panel, `ul.dates .name` is nowrap with ellipsis above 600 px (the wrap rule is only under `max-width: 600px`), so a long
family-date name is cut at 640 px as well (measured: scrollWidth 400 vs clientWidth 333). The panel at 320 px wraps correctly and has no
horizontal scroll. Screen-reader users are not affected (the DOM holds the full text); low-vision users who zoom are.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Dashboard with the card (`days: 60, limit: 8`), he, `qa_admin`. Set the viewport to 320 x 568 (or 640 px wide = 200% zoom of 1280).
2. Look at the Shmini Atzeret item: truncated with an ellipsis. Click it: the title is not in the details.
3. Panel, family dates, 640 px wide: the row "יום הנישואין של סבא וסבתא משפחת כהן-לוי מרמת גן ותל אביב" is cut.

## Expected

All content readable at 200% zoom and at 320 px without horizontal scrolling (1.4.4, 1.4.10): wrap the name, or make the full text available on
the same screen.

## Actual

```
card 320 / 640 : .name scrollWidth 169 > clientWidth 141, title=null ("שמיני עצרת · שמחת תורה · שבת")
panel 640       : .name scrollWidth 400 > clientWidth 333, title=null
expanded details: "כ״ב תשרי תשפ״ז · הדלקת נרות 17:54 · הבדלה 18:59" + "לוח שנה: חגי ישראל"  (no title)
```

## Evidence

Playwright 1.63 / Chromium 153 measurements (`gd-J.mjs`, `gd-K.mjs`). No horizontal page overflow at 320 or 640 in panel or card.

## Likely root cause

Likely: `.name { white-space: nowrap; text-overflow: ellipsis }` kept for compact rows; the panel's wrap override starts at 600 px instead of applying whenever the name would not fit.

## Relevant files

`custom_components/good_days/www/card.js` STYLE `.name`; `custom_components/good_days/www/panel.js` STYLE `.name` and the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block.

## Recommended remediation

Let `.name` wrap (`white-space: normal; overflow-wrap: anywhere`), at least in the card below ~400 px container width and in the panel at all widths. Keep the compact one-line card variant ellipsised but add `title`.

## Regression test

A11Y-001 / MOBILE-001. Layout measurement, not something a unit test holds.
