## Summary

"נוסח ההפטרה" is the fifth field, below the map, latitude / longitude, diaspora switch and the candle and havdalah minutes. Options: "אשכנז" (preselected) and "ספרד / עדות המזרח". The description "איזו הפטרה תוצג לכל שבת." only repeats the label and renders under the second radio. Nothing says the customs differ on 16 of 61 weekly parashot (plus several special Shabbatot), so a user cannot tell whether the setting matters, and nothing on the card shows which custom is displayed. The default is Ashkenazi for everyone, including Israeli households where a large share are Sephardi / Edot HaMizrach: a wrong default gives a plausible but wrong citation about one week in four. The wording itself is clear and correct.

## Steps to reproduce

Settings, Devices, Good Days, configure; scroll past the map.

Evidence: `qa/evidence/UX-016-nusach-option-1440.png`. Count from `haftarah_data.py` (PARASHA entries where the two customs differ: 16 of 61).

![UX-016-nusach-option-1440](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-016-nusach-option-1440.png?raw=1)

## Expected

(Recommendation.) Keep or change the default as the owner decides, but make the description informative ("ההבדלים בין הנוסחים הם בכרבע מהשבתות; בשאר הן זהות"), and show the custom beside the citation in card details where the customs differ.
