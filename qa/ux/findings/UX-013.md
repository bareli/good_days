## Problem

After #21 the compact line picks the right item: "חול המועד סוכות · הבא: שמיני עצרת · שמחת תורה · שבת · מחר ·
הדלקת נרות 17:54". It is one line with an ellipsis, and the candle-lighting time is its **last** part. At 800 px
it fits. On every common phone width it does not: the line needs 437 px and gets 292 px (360), 322 px (390) and
344 px (412). What remains is "חול המועד סוכות · הבא: שמיני עצרת · שמחת תורה · שבת · מ…". The time, the one thing
the line exists for, is cut away. The full text is only in a `title` tooltip, which a touch screen does not show.

## Why it matters

Same user problem as #21, on the phone: the compact card is chosen to answer "when do we light" in one glance, and
on a phone it answers with the holiday names instead. It is worst in the weeks with the longest titles (Sukkot,
Pesach, Shavuot + Shabbat), which are the weeks the times matter most.

## Evidence

390x800, light, Hebrew, `/gd-qa/compact` (first card `{compact: true}`), 1 Oct 2026 (Chol HaMoed Sukkot). The full
card underneath shows the time the compact line loses:

![compact line on a phone](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/fix/qa-2026-09-30/qa/evidence/UX-013-compact-phone-390.png?raw=1)

Measured `scrollWidth > clientWidth` on the ellipsised element: 437 > 292 (360), 437 > 322 (390), 437 > 344 (412).

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner: put the time before the title so the ellipsis eats the title, not the time:
"הדלקת נרות 17:54 · מחר · שמיני עצרת · שמחת תורה · שבת", and drop the in-progress prefix ("חול המועד סוכות ·")
when the line does not fit (or show it only at wider widths). Alternative: allow the compact line to wrap to two
lines below about 480 px. en: "Candles 17:54 · tomorrow · Shemini Atzeret ...".

## Kind

Works, could be better (the #21 fix is correct; the order of the parts does not survive a narrow screen).

## Prototype

Not offered; a text-order change.
