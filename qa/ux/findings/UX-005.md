## Problem

Every timer row has **הפעלה עכשיו**, and its accessible name is "הפעלת פלטה (כיבוי) עכשיו". In Hebrew,
"הפעלה" is the everyday word for switching a device on (the same word the product uses for its on/off chip
"הדלקה"). On the row "פלטה (כיבוי)" the button reads as "turn the hot plate on now", while it actually runs the
timer, which turns the hot plate **off**. The screen reader label says "turn on hot plate (off) now".

## Why it matters

The hot plate is a device people do not want switched the wrong way by mistake. The label should name the
effect, not the mechanism.

## Evidence

![run-now button on the off timer](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-005-run-now-on-off-timer.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner (copy):

- Button: "לבדוק עכשיו" or name the effect: "לכבות עכשיו" / "להדליק עכשיו" by the rule's action.
- Accessible name: "לבדוק את פלטה (כיבוי) עכשיו: יכבה את פלטה".
- en: "Test now" (aria: "Test Hot plate (off) now: turns Hot plate off").
