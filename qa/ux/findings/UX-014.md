## Summary

The haftarah appears only after tapping a Shabbat row, as one item in a dot-separated line ("29 Tishrei 5787 · Machar Chodesh · ... · Haftarah: I Samuel 20:18-42 · Candle lighting 17:45 · Havdalah 18:51"). The collapsed row and the blue "next" hero box show parasha, candle lighting and havdalah only. Rows have no chevron or other sign that they open; the hero box does not expand at all; the compact card never shows the haftarah. The expanded line also repeats the candle and havdalah times already on the row, so the new information is its least prominent part.

Why it matters: the family member who wants the haftarah will not tap a row that looks static, and a wall tablet is glanced at, not operated. The headline v0.7 feature is invisible by default.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Dashboard with `custom:good-days-card` (categories shabbat), Hebrew UI.
2. Look at the card: no haftarah anywhere. Tap a Shabbat row: the haftarah appears inside the details line.

Evidence: `qa/evidence/UX-014-haftarah-hidden-collapsed-1440.png` (collapsed, nothing) and `qa/evidence/UX-014-haftarah-expanded-320.png` (after tap, 320 wide).

## Expected

(Recommendation, not a requirement.) A Shabbat row with a haftarah shows a visible secondary line under the parasha ("הפטרה: ישעיהו נד:א–נה:ה"), at least in the hero box; a card option (for example `show_haftarah`, default on) and a chevron on expandable rows. Product call on default density.

## Actual

Works, could be better. Hidden until an unmarked tap, buried in a run-on line.
