## Summary

Combined nitpicks:

1. Hebrew verse letters have no geresh / gershayim while the Hebrew date beside them does ("ו׳ סיון" vs "כז:ו"); a lone ו or ב after a colon reads like a conjunction.
2. Two-part haftarot repeat the book: "ישעיהו כז:ו–כח:יג; ישעיהו כט:כב–כג". "ישעיהו כז:ו–כח:יג, כט:כב–כג" is shorter.
3. At 320 the label separates from the citation: "הפטרה:" ends one line, the citation starts the next.
4. Machar Chodesh Shabbat (Bereshit, 10 Oct 2026) shows I Samuel 20:18-42 with "Machar Chodesh" earlier in the line but not that it replaces the weekly haftarah; someone comparing with the printed Bereshit haftarah will think it is wrong.

Bidi is fine: with Hebrew letters only, colon, en dash and semicolon stay in reading order in RTL at 1440 and 320, and in English.
