## Problem

For `qa_user` the tab renders the same layout as for an admin, with the master checkbox, the skip checkbox and
the profile select disabled (greyed) and the row actions removed. Nothing says the page is read-only or why.
A disabled checkbox with no reason looks broken; the user tries it again or assumes the feature does not work.
(Read access itself works and the preview is useful to them.)

## Why it matters

Non-admin household members are the everyday users (PROJECT.md *Users*). "Skip this Shabbat" is the control
they are most likely to want, and they have no way of knowing they should ask the admin.

## Evidence

![non-admin timers tab](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-007-non-admin-timers.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner (copy): a one-line notice at the top of the tab for non-admins:
"רק מנהלי Home Assistant יכולים לשנות טיימרים. אפשר לראות כאן מה יופעל." /
en "Only Home Assistant admins can change timers. You can see here what will run."
Whether non-admins should be allowed to skip a Shabbat is a product decision (see also SEC-001, which is about
the entities, not this screen).
