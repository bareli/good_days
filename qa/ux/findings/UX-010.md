## Problem

The yahrzeit "יום השנה: סבא משה (17)" (ל׳ חשוון; in 5787 Cheshvan has 30 days, so it falls on ל׳ חשוון = Tue 10.11.2026 and is not moved) appears under
"יום שני 9.11 · 29 בחשוון" with a lone **16:43** under the title. Shabbat rows say "הדלקת נרות 17:54"; this one
has no word at all, and it uses the same candle icon as the Shabbat rows. A reader cannot tell whether 16:43 is
the time to light, the start, or a reminder. The header date (29 Cheshvan) also differs from the date the user
entered (30 Cheshvan) with no explanation: the header is the evening the yahrzeit begins (sunset on 29 Cheshvan),
which nothing on the row says. (Corrected 2026-10-01 at verification: the first version of this record said the
30th-day rule moved the date to א׳ כסלו; it does not in 5787, see #29.)

## Why it matters

Lighting the yahrzeit candle before sunset is the one action tied to this row; the time should say so.

## Evidence

![yahrzeit row with bare time](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-010-yahrzeit-bare-time.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner (copy): "מתחיל בשקיעה 16:43 · הדלקת נר נשמה לפני 16:43"; when the rule moved the
date, add "(ל׳ חשוון חל השנה בא׳ כסלו)". en: "Begins at sunset 16:43".
