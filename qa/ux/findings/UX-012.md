## Summary

With two Good Days entries the panel shows its instance picker (correct), but both options read "Good Days": the
config flow always titles a new entry "Good Days" and the picker labels options with `entry.title`. The user cannot
tell Israel from New York unless they know to rename the entry in Settings.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Add a second Good Days entry from Settings (any other location).
2. Open the Good Days panel; open the picker at the top.

## Expected

Distinguishable options. My inference from SPEC §7.2 ("Allowed (e.g. two locations)"); not written anywhere.

## Actual

Two options, both "Good Days". The card editor's entity picker has the same ambiguity (two "חגים / Good Days").

## Evidence

`config_entries/get` domain good_days: both entries `"title": "Good Days"`. `config_flow.py`:
`self.async_create_entry(title="Good Days", ...)`.

## Screenshots

1280 px, light theme, Hebrew. The select was given `size=2` in the page only so both options show in one image
(native dropdowns are not captured in screenshots).

![two identical Good Days options](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-012-identical-instance-options.png?raw=1)

## Relevant files

- `custom_components/good_days/config_flow.py` (entry title)
- `custom_components/good_days/www/panel.js` (instance picker, `new Option(e.title, e.entry_id)`)

## Recommended remediation

Owner decision: title new entries distinctly (coordinates, "Good Days 2", or location plus diaspora flag) and / or
show the location in the picker label.
