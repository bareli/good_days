## Problem

The checkbox label names the period well ("דילוג על שמיני עצרת · שמחת תורה · שבת"). After ticking it, the
preview adds an orange line "דילוג: שום דבר לא יופעל בשבת / חג הזה." but **the same card still lists the three
actions with their times and green / grey chips exactly as before**. The message and the rows disagree.
Nothing on screen says the skip clears itself after this Shabbat (README: "resets by itself"), so a careful
user will come back on Sunday to untick it, or worry that it is still on.

## Why it matters

A skip is set in a hurry before leaving. The user needs to see at a glance that the hot plate will not come on.

## Evidence

![skip on: message plus unchanged rows](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-006-skip-preview.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner:

1. When skipped, render that period's rows struck through / dimmed with "לא יופעל" instead of the action chip.
2. Hint under the checkbox: "חל רק על השבת / החג הזה, ומתבטל מעצמו אחריו."
3. en: "Applies to this Shabbat / Chag only and clears itself afterwards."

## Kind

Works, could be better.
