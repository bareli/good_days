## Problem

A fresh card shows Shabbatot, Yom Tov, Chol HaMoed, minor days and (when in range) family dates, yet every
checkbox in the editor is empty, including "תאריכים משפחתיים". The rule "ריק = הגדרות האינטגרציה" is only in
the group label. Ticking one box (say "שבת") silently switches from "everything" to "only Shabbat", so the
first click hides most of the card, including family dates the user never unticked.

## Why it matters

Checkbox state that contradicts what is on screen makes users distrust the editor; the first edit usually
produces a surprising result.

## Evidence

![editor: all unchecked, preview shows them](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-009-editor-categories.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner: when `categories` is unset, render the boxes checked according to the integration
options + family (the effective set), and add a single toggle above them "לפי הגדרות ימים טובים" (on by
default) that, when turned off, makes the boxes editable. en: "Use Good Days settings".
