## Summary

The preset dialog is a select of eight names plus the generic hint "יוצרת טיימר הדלקה וטיימר כיבוי; אפשר לערוך את שניהם". It does not say what the chosen preset does; for "דוד חשמל" the admin sees "on 12:00 Erev, off 15 min before candle lighting" only after saving and reading two rules. Applies-to (all three include Yom Kippur) is also invisible.

Checked in the live preview after creating the three presets (Shabbat 9-10 Oct 2026; created on dev and removed afterwards): water heater on Fri 12:00, off 17:30 (candle lighting 17:45); porch light on 17:35, off Sat 01:00; bedroom AC on Fri 22:00, off Sat 06:30. Times and names ("דוד חשמל (הדלקה)" / "(כיבוי)") make sense and the preview reads well at 320. Next to Yom Tov (Shmini Atzeret run): heater on only on the Erev of the run, so later days of a multi-day run get no heating; correct by design but unstated.

Panel, Shabbat timers, "מתבנית".
