## Summary

When Yom Tov and Shabbat form one run, the title is the holiday ("שבועות · שבת" / "Shavuot · Shabbat") and the details say "הפטרה: שופטים יג:ב–כה" / "Haftarah: Judges 13:2-25". That is the haftarah of Shabbat Naso, not of Shavuot, but nothing says so: no parasha name, no "Shabbat". A reader takes it for the holiday's haftarah (event of 10 June 2027). When the Shabbat is itself Yom Tov (Shmini Atzeret / Simchat Torah, 3 Oct 2026) the sensor has `parasha: null, haftarah: null` and nothing explains the gap; Shabbat Chol HaMoed shows nothing either (README: "no haftarah yet").

Why it matters: a wrong reading is worse than a missing one; someone may prepare Samson's story for Shavuot.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Card `{days: 400, limit: 100, categories: [shabbat, yom_tov]}`, expand the "שבועות · שבת" item (or call `good_days/upcoming` with days 400).
2. Read the details line.
