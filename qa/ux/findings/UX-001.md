## Problem

The two times a Shabbat household actually plans around are candle lighting and havdalah. On the card both
lose to everything else:

- Candle lighting is rendered at **11.2 px, grey (rgb 94,94,94)**, below the title, with a small candle icon.
- The countdown chip is 10.9 px; the Hebrew date 10.9 px grey.
- **Havdalah is not on the card at all** unless you tap the item (details line: "הדלקת נרות 17:54 · הבדלה 18:59").
- Every row has the same weight: "חול המועד סוכות · עכשיו", "הושענא רבה", Shemini Atzeret + Shabbat, and five
  plain Shabbatot 9 to 30 days away all look alike, so the eye has no entry point.

Measured on 2026-09-30 (Chol HaMoed Sukkot), 800 px: the card is a 490 px column; the next Shabbat / Yom Tov
(Shemini Atzeret · Simchat Torah · Shabbat, Fri 17:54 to Sat 18:59) is the third row.

## Why it matters

The card's promise (README: "what's coming up ... with a live countdown") is a kitchen-tablet glance. At 1-2 m,
11 px grey text is not readable, and the one question asked every Friday ("עד מתי מדליקים? מתי יוצאת השבת?")
needs a tap plus reading a small details line. On a touch tablet that is often in kiosk mode, the tap is the
friction.

## Evidence

Computed styles (800 px): `.name` 13.3 px; candle-lighting span 11.2 px rgb(94,94,94); `.chip` 10.92 px;
`.hdate` 10.92 px rgb(94,94,94).

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner:

1. A summary block at the top of the card for the current or next Shabbat / Chag only:
   title, "הדלקת נרות 17:54 · הבדלה 18:59" at title size or larger, and the countdown ("בעוד יומיים").
   During Shabbat it becomes the existing "שבת שלום" banner with the havdalah time.
2. In the list, show havdalah next to candle lighting for timed Shabbat / Yom Tov rows
   ("הדלקת נרות 17:54 · הבדלה 18:59"), and raise secondary text to at least 12-13 px with body-colour times.
3. Optional card option `font_scale` or a "tablet" size for wall displays.

## Kind

Works, could be better. Nothing is wrong in the data; the hierarchy does not serve the main glance task.
