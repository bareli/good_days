## Problem

Compact mode is documented as "Next: Shabbat · in 2 days · candles 17:52". On 2026-09-30 (Chol HaMoed Sukkot)
it reads **"הבא: חול המועד סוכות · עכשיו"** ("Next: Chol HaMoed Sukkot · now"): it says "next" for something
that is already happening, and hides the thing the line exists for, Shemini Atzeret / Shabbat with candle
lighting Fri 17:54, two days away.

`_renderCompact` shows `items[0]`, and an all-day event in progress is always first. The same happens for all
eight days of Chanukah, every Chol HaMoed day, Rosh Chodesh (when enabled) and any all-day external event.

## Why it matters

Someone who put the compact card on the tablet to know "when do we light this week" gets no candle time
for about 20 days a year, including the weeks with the most complex schedules (Sukkot, Pesach, Chanukah).

## Evidence

![compact card on Chol HaMoed](https://github.com/bareli/good_days/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-002-compact-chol-hamoed.png?raw=1)

Top: the compact card. Below it, a full card on the same dashboard shows the next Shabbat / Chag with 17:54.

## Recommendation

Proposal for the owner: in compact mode, prefer the next item that has `candle_lighting` (or the current
Shabbat / Yom Tov while `in_effect`), and only fall back to `items[0]` if there is none. If an all-day
holiday is in progress, it can lead the line as a prefix: "חול המועד סוכות · הבא: שמיני עצרת · בעוד יומיים ·
הדלקת נרות 17:54". Never combine "הבא" with "עכשיו".

## Kind

Current behaviour is wrong against the documented intent of compact mode (README *The card*).
